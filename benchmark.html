Benchmarks

Year Term Benchmark
1 Fall

  • Choose an overarching topic for the Ph.D dissertation

  • Basic quantitative/computational skills (BIOSTATS)

    • Project management in Rstudio

    • Basic operations in Git/Github

    • Full competency in base & tidyverse operations (Chapters 4 - 6)

    • Sampling/probability theory, likelihood, two-group test, multi-group test, GLM framework (Chapters 7 - 16)

  • Basic GIS skills (R-GIS for Ecology)

    • Coordinate Reference Systems

    • Vector data operations

    • Raster data operations

  • Reading

    • Be familiar with your research topic (read 30 articles in the research area at minimum)
Spring

  • Develop (tentative) objectives for all chapters

  • Develop a plan for summer data collection (if applicable)

  • Advanced statistical skills

    • GLMM, GAM, multi-variate analysis (PCA, PCoA, NMDS, etc), time-series analysis, structural equation modeling

  • Advanced GIS skills

    • Watershed analysis

  • Reading

    • Focused reading to be able to explain the “big-picture” in your own words. This reading should be concept-oriented, not taxon-specific. How different is your work from previous similar studies? Is the “difference” significant (non-trivial)? How broad is the attention your research may get?

    • Focused reading to identify the suitable sampling/experimental design of the study taxa for the upcoming data-collection season.
Summer

  • Begin main data collection

    • Complete data collection for a chapter or two

    • Sample processing

    • Organize data in an analyzable format
2 Fall

  • Revisit objectives for all chapters, adjust plans as needed

  • Complete the preliminary analysis of the collected data

  • Improve and expand analytical skills

  • Reading

    • Focused reading to be able to explain the “big-picture” in your own words.

    • Expanded reading on broader research areas.
Spring

  • Complete required coursework and proposal defense

  • Develop a plan for summer data collection (if applicable)

  • Improve and expand analytical skills

  • Reading

    • Focused reading to be able to explain the “big-picture” in your own words.

    • Focused reading to identify the suitable sampling/experimental design of the study taxa for the upcoming data-collection season.

    • Expanded reading on broader research topics.
Summer
  • Data collection
    • Complete data collection for all chapters
    • Sample processing
    • Organize data in an analyzable format
3 Fall

  • Complete analysis and begin writing a manuscript for the first chapter

  • Improve and expand analytical skills

  • Reading

    • Focused reading to be able to explain the novelty of the chapter.

    • Expanded reading on broader research areas.
Spring

  • Submit a manuscript of the first chapter

  • Improve and expand analytical skills

  • Reading

    • Focused reading to be able to explain the novelty of the chapter.

    • Expanded reading on broader research areas.
Summer

  • Complete analysis and begin writing a manuscript for the second chapter

  • Supplemental data collection, if any
4 Fall

  • Submit the manuscript of the second chapter

  • Complete analysis and begin writing a manuscript for the final chapter
Spring

  • Submit the manuscript of the final chapter

  • Write dissertation and defend
